Processed IPIP Big Five Dataset -- Codebook
=============================================

Personality item scale: 1=Disagree, 2=Slightly Disagree, 3=Neutral,
                        4=Slightly Agree, 5=Agree

Each item column holds the participant's RAW answer to the statement (1-5). Reverse-scored items were only flipped temporarily to compute the OCEAN trait scores used to build the target; the values stored here match the literal question.

------------------

N1   I get stressed out easily.
N2   I am relaxed most of the time.
N3   I worry about things.
N4   I seldom feel blue.
N5   I am easily disturbed.
N6   I get upset easily.
N7   I change my mood a lot.
N8   I have frequent mood swings.
N9   I get irritated easily.
N10  I often feel blue.
E1   I am the life of the party.
E3   I feel comfortable around people.
E4   I keep in the background.
E5   I start conversations.
E7   I talk to a lot of different people at parties.
E9   I don't mind being the center of attention.
E10  I am quiet around strangers.
C4   I make a mess of things.
A4   I sympathize with others' feelings.

Demographic Columns
--------------------

age    Age in years (numeric).
gender Categorical: 'Male', 'Female', 'Other'
hand   Categorical: 'Right', 'Left', 'Both'

Target Column
--------------

target  Personality type derived from OCEAN z-scores:

        - Moderate        Default (when no other rule matches)
        - Resilient       Neuroticism (N) < -0.5
        - Overcontroller  Extraversion (E) < -0.5 AND Neuroticism (N) > 0.5
        - Undercontroller Conscientiousness (C) < -0.2 AND Agreeableness (A) < -0.2
        - Reserved        Extraversion (E) < -0.5 AND Neuroticism (N) < -0.5
